# Pose library

Pick a pose per shot, then paste its sentence into the prompt and add its visibility clause. Use each pose at most once per ~10 photos.

Every pose carries attitude, and that is what makes the photo "influencer" rather than "person":
- a pout, a smirk, a knowing half-smile, or a cool unbothered look;
- looking past the lens or straight into it with confidence.

Hands always have a job: sunglasses, hair, a coffee, a bag strap, a lapel, a pocket. Dead hands hanging at the sides read as catalogue.

Risk notes say what tends to break. The gate in SKILL.md still applies.

Poses are written as "she". For men, switch the pronouns and drop anything that reads feminine (a pout, a hip pop, nails). The "men" tags and the X section are the best starts for men.

## M: Mirror selfies

The phone sits beside the face or covers part of it. Show the mirror's frame at an angle, never square-on.

## W: Walking and street

Shoot at three-quarters or diagonally across the frame, not down the middle of the street.

| id | Pose sentence | Keep visible | Good for / risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | Walks diagonally across the frame mid-stride, one hand lifting the edge of her sunglasses, chin up, looking past the camera. | "Her stride is open so both legs and both shoes read." | Any. The influencer default. |
| W2 | Crosses on a zebra crossing toward the kerb, bag swinging from the hand, glancing back over her shoulder with a half-smile. | Front three-quarter, not a back view. | Trousers, coats. |
| W3 | Steps off the kerb with a takeaway coffee held up near her face, the other hand in her coat pocket, laughing at someone off camera. | The coffee stays at the side, not over the top. | Coats, knits. |
| W4 | Walks toward the camera with wind in her hair, one hand catching strands, the coat flaring open. | "The coat swings open but does not cover the trousers." | Long coats, scarves. |
| W5 | Mid-stride with the bag hooked in the crook of the elbow, the other arm swinging, a confident runway walk. | The bag stays at the elbow, away from the skirt. | Top-handle bags, skirts. |
| W6 | Fashion-week street style: caught by a photographer crouching low, walking past a black car, adjusting a cuff. | Long lens, background blur allowed. | Any; strong for men. |
| W7 | Walks along a market or flower stall, glancing at the flowers, one hand trailing near a bucket of tulips. | The full outfit in front of the stall. | Boho, romantic, spring-like colours. |
| W8 | Walks while looking down at her phone, a slight smile, the bag on her shoulder, the other hand in her pocket. | The phone at chest height, not covering the top. | Downtown, casual. |
| W9 | Twirls mid-step so the skirt flares, laughing, the arms slightly out. | The skirt flare readable, the shoes in frame. | Midi and maxi skirts, party. Risk: motion blur. |
| W10 | Walks past a café terrace with a baguette or a small paper bag under her arm, looking ahead. | The prop stays under the arm, not over the top. | Parisian, old money. |

## P: Waist-up portraits and POV

Use these only when the hero pieces are on top (jacket, knit, jewelry, hat) or on the bottom (trousers, shoes) in POV. They are about 10% of a set at most.
