# Four approved prompts

These four were approved by the art director after four rejected rounds, and the whole batch followed them. Each was sent with its reference images in this order: one per garment (image 1..), then the bag, then the small-accessories board. A real reference photo, when one was used, went last.

## Winter Bimbo #1: wavy-mirror selfie (an angled home mirror selfie as the last reference)

```
An outfit mirror selfie posted by a y2k it-girl fashion influencer and saved on Pinterest, the kind that makes you want the whole look.
She takes it on her iPhone in a tall floor mirror with a wavy, squiggly cream frame leaning against the wall of her cosy bedroom in the evening. The photo is NOT straight-on: she stands at an angle to the mirror, so the wavy frame cuts diagonally through the image, the room recedes sideways, the floorboards and the rug run at a slant, and the whole picture is tilted a few degrees. The mirror's frame edge shows on one side.
She pops one hip, one knee bent inward, and holds the phone in a plain pink case at chin height, beside her face so her face still shows. Her free hand plays with the bag strap. She gives the mirror a confident, flirty pout.
She is a gorgeous Latina in her early 20s with a big glossy blowout in dark brown with honey highlights, full lashes, a glossy pink lip, bronzed glowing skin and long almond nails.

She wears exactly these pieces from the reference images, fitting her perfectly. They are the hero of the photo:
- the pale pink faux-fur cropped jacket with a collar (image 1), worn open, both arms through the sleeves;
- the white baby tee with black ringer trim (image 2);
- the light faded-blue low-rise bootcut jeans (image 3), long and flared over the boots;
- the chestnut suede mini boots (image 4);
- the pink snakeskin shoulder bag (image 5) on her shoulder;
- from image 6: the hot-pink skinny oval sunglasses pushed up on her head, the silver rhinestone hoops, and the silver rhinestone bow choker.
Everything is visible in the reflection from head to boots.

The room: a slightly unmade bed with white linen and a pink throw, a mushroom table lamp glowing warm, pink tulips in a glass vase, a stack of magazines on the floor, a vintage rug, a few photos taped to the wall.
Light: the warm table lamp and a little cool evening light from the window, soft shadows.
The photo looks like a real iPhone mirror selfie: slight grain in the darker corners, true colours (pale pink fur, white, faded blue denim, chestnut suede, hot pink), natural skin texture.
The last image is only a reference for the angled mirror framing and photo quality; do not copy that person, their face, their clothes or the room.
CRITICAL: no straight-on symmetrical composition, no studio, no plastic or airbrushed CGI skin, no readable text on the phone or anywhere.
```

## Soft Grunge #1: Soho flash at blue hour (a night street walk as the last reference). This one is straighter-on than ideal; add an angle phrase when reusing it

```
An outfit post by a fashion influencer on Instagram, the kind of photo that makes you want to buy the whole look. Her photographer friend shot it on an iPhone at blue hour, flash on, from slightly below waist height, so she looks tall and fills most of the frame from the beanie to the boots, a little left of centre.
She stands on a cobblestone street in downtown Manhattan in front of a black-painted bar facade with warm amber light glowing through the window. Her weight is on one leg, one hand on her hip pushing the jacket back, the other holding the bag strap, chin slightly down. She looks straight into the lens with total confidence and a slight smirk.
She is a striking alt-girl fashion influencer in her mid 20s with a curvy, confident figure, glossy bubblegum-pink hair in a sleek blunt cut falling from under the beanie, flawless but real skin, sharp black winged liner, a dark-lined glossy lip and silver rings on her fingers.

She wears exactly these pieces from the reference images, styled with intention and fitting her perfectly. They are the hero of the photo:
- the black leather biker jacket with silver zips and a belted hem (image 1), worn open, both arms through the sleeves, sleeves pushed up a little;
- the sheer black tulle long-sleeve top (image 2), exactly as in the reference;
- the dark burgundy-brown and black plaid mini skirt with a patch pocket (image 3);
- the black leather knee-high biker boots with a slouchy folded shaft and a low block heel (image 4), clearly knee-high;
- the distressed dark brown leather flap shoulder bag (image 5);
- from image 6: the fuzzy black wool beanie, the chunky silver chain necklace over the top, and the silver linked hoop earrings.
She also wears sheer black tights.
Every piece is visible from head to boots.

Light: the iPhone flash lights her crisply from the front against the deep blue dusk, with warm bar light in the background and a glint on the leather and the silver.
The photo looks like a real influencer's iPhone outfit shot edited with a clean, slightly warm preset: rich blacks, true colours, sharp on her, natural skin texture.
The last image is only a reference for pose, framing and photo quality; do not copy that person, their face or their clothes.
CRITICAL: no studio backdrop, no catalogue stiffness, no plastic or airbrushed CGI skin, no readable brand names or text.
```
