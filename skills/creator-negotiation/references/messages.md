# Message skeletons

Short skeletons for the situations that come up in a creator negotiation, in English and Spanish. They're starting points: rewrite them in the creator's tone (match their emoji, their warmth, their length), fill in real facts, and drop any line that isn't true for you. Every one of them is one message with one number at most.

Sign with a real first name and one line about the product, e.g. `Alex / yourapp.com - budgeting app for students`.

## 1. Accept their package price

Use when they offered a series price you can afford. Take it as offered. Add only what makes the deal easier for them.

**EN**
> Hi {name}, thank you for such a complete answer! The package of 3 videos for {price} works for us. We'd pay each video upfront, before you film it, via PayPal, and we cover the fees. The videos are yours, in your usual style, each one showing a different side of the app. If that works, I'll send a one-page agreement and a few ideas (just ideas).

**ES**
> ¡Hola {nombre}! Gracias por la info tan completa. El paquete de 3 videos por {precio} nos funciona. Te pagamos cada video por adelantado, antes de grabarlo, por PayPal, y las comisiones las cubrimos nosotros. Los videos son tuyos, en tu estilo, cada uno mostrando una parte distinta de la app. Si te parece bien, te mando un acuerdo de una página y algunas ideas (solo ideas).

## 2. Counter by trading scope

Use when your ceiling is 0.33–0.6 of their per-video ask. One number and the reason for it. The scope change is how you meet them, not a smaller price for the same work.

**EN**
> Hi {name}, thank you, and I love how you described the angle for your audience. Being upfront: the package is more than we can commit to right now. Would you be open to starting with one video? We could do {price} for one TikTok, paid upfront via PayPal. If it goes well, we'd love to do the other two. And if it doesn't work for you, no hard feelings at all.

**ES**
> ¡Hola {nombre}! Gracias, me encantó cómo lo planteas para tu audiencia. Te soy sincera: el paquete ahora mismo es más de lo que podemos. ¿Te animarías a empezar con un video? Podríamos pagar {precio} por un TikTok, por adelantado por PayPal. Si sale bien, nos encantaría hacer los otros dos. Y si no te encaja, no pasa nada.

In the 0.6–1 band, send the same message with your ceiling as the price and no scope change.

Scope levers, in the order to give them up: cross-post to Reels/Shorts → number of videos (test one first) → series length. Leave creative freedom and upfront payment alone; they're what makes a lower number fair.

## 3. Say yes to their counter

Use when they answered your offer with a number at or under your ceiling. Say yes clearly; don't counter the counter.

**EN**
> Deal, {price} works for us. Thank you for working with our budget! The agreement is attached. To confirm, just reply "I agree", and send your PayPal email so the first payment goes out today.

**ES**
> ¡Trato hecho, {precio} nos funciona! Gracias por adaptarte a nuestro presupuesto. Te adjunto el acuerdo: para confirmar, responde «de acuerdo» y mándame tu correo de PayPal, y el primer pago sale hoy.

## 4. Confirm the currency

Use whenever a LatAm creator writes "$" without a currency, or the number only makes sense in local money. Never pick the reading that's cheaper for you without asking.

**EN**
> Thank you for sharing your rates! Just to confirm: are the prices in {local currency}? If so, the package works for us and we'd love to start. If they're in US dollars, it's unfortunately more than we can do at this stage.

**ES**
> ¡Gracias por compartir tus tarifas! Solo para confirmar: ¿los precios son en {pesos mexicanos}? Si es así, el paquete nos funciona y nos encantaría arrancar. Si fueran dólares, lamentablemente es más de lo que podemos en esta etapa.

## 5. Ask for the audience screenshot

Use before paying anyone whose audience geography you can't see. Say why; it's a fair question and creators answer it the same day.

**EN**
> Before we confirm: could you send a screenshot of your audience by country (TikTok Analytics → Viewers)? Our app mostly pays back in {countries}, so it helps us plan the series.

**ES**
> Antes de confirmar, ¿me podrías mandar una captura de tu audiencia por países (TikTok Analytics → Espectadores)? La app funciona sobre todo en {países}, así que nos ayuda a planear la serie.

## 6. Warm decline (the gap is too big)

Use when your ceiling is under about a third of their ask. Don't counter. Their rate is probably fair; your budget is the reason.

**EN**
> Hi {name}, thank you for the quick and clear answer, really appreciate it! Honestly, {rate} is well beyond what we can do at this stage, and I don't want to waste your time with a number that far off. We'd love to come back to you once we've grown. Good luck with your content!

**ES**
> ¡Hola {nombre}! Gracias por responder tan claro, se agradece de verdad. Te soy sincera: {tarifa} ahora mismo está muy por encima de lo que podemos, y no quiero hacerte perder el tiempo con una cifra tan lejana. Si crecemos y podemos acercarnos, te escribo la primera. ¡Mucha suerte con todo!

## 7. Close after they hold their number

Use when they answered your honest offer with the same rate ("I'd prefer to keep my minimum"). Accept their answer; don't send a second number.

**EN**
> Totally understand, and thank you for being so honest. I'll reach out when our budget grows. Wishing you lots of success with your content!

**ES**
> Lo entiendo perfectamente, y gracias por tu sinceridad. Te escribo en cuanto crezca nuestro presupuesto. ¡Mucho éxito con tu contenido!

## 8. One check-in after silence

Use once, 5–7 days after your last message. Give explicit permission to say no. If this one gets no answer, close the thread in your notes and move on.

**EN**
> Hi {name}, just checking my last message didn't get buried. If the timing or the budget doesn't work, a quick "no" is totally fine!

**ES**
> ¡Hola {nombre}! Te escribo por si se te pasó mi mensaje. Si ahora no es buen momento o el presupuesto no te encaja, ¡un «no» también está perfecto!

## 9. Keep it in writing (translator)

Use when a creator asks for a call in a language you don't speak. Tell the truth about the translator; offer a call in a shared language as the alternative.

**EN**
> Happy to hear you already have ideas! One thing first: I write to you with a translator, so I'd rather keep talking here by email, where nothing gets lost. Tell me what you'd like to adjust and your ideas, I'd love to read them. If you'd still prefer a call and English works for you, we can do {day/time in their time zone}.

**ES**
> ¡Qué bueno que ya tienes ideas! Un detalle antes: no hablo español, te escribo con un traductor, así que prefiero seguir por correo, donde no se pierde nada. Cuéntame qué te gustaría ajustar y tus ideas, ¡me encantará leerlas! Si de todos modos prefieres una llamada y te funciona en inglés, podemos el {día/hora en su zona}.

## 10. Payment method they asked for

Use when they ask for bank transfer, Interac, etc. Offer the default first with its real advantage, then accommodate.

**EN**
> On payment: PayPal is the easiest for us, since the money arrives the same day and we cover the fees. Would that work for you? If it really doesn't, we can try a bank transfer. In that case, send me the account holder name, bank name, account number and SWIFT code, and I'll set it up (an international transfer can take a few days).

**ES**
> Sobre el pago: para nosotros PayPal es lo más fácil, el dinero llega el mismo día y cubrimos las comisiones. ¿Te funciona? Si de verdad no, probamos con transferencia bancaria: en ese caso mándame titular, banco, número de cuenta y código SWIFT (una transferencia internacional puede tardar unos días).

## 11. Results first, then the next video

Use with per-video deals when you want to see the first video perform before paying for the second. It's honest only if the agreement pays per video.

**EN**
> Thank you, it looks great! And it's already working: people are signing up with your code. Let's give it a couple of days to see how it does, and I'll get back to you about the second video.

**ES**
> ¡Gracias, quedó genial! Y ya está funcionando: hay gente registrándose con tu código. Démosle un par de días para ver cómo va y te escribo sobre el segundo video.

## 12. A small discount when their price already works

Use when your ceiling is at or above their ask. Ask once, tie it to something real you give, and say yes in the same message so a "no" costs nothing. If they decline, accept their price in your next message; don't ask again.

**EN**
> Hi {name}, thank you! {price} works for us. One question, and it's completely fine if the answer is no: if we pay the full amount upfront (or book the series of 3), is there any flexibility on the rate? Either way, we'd love to go ahead.

**ES**
> ¡Hola {nombre}! Gracias, {precio} nos funciona. Una pregunta, y está perfecto si la respuesta es no: si pagamos todo por adelantado (o hacemos la serie de 3), ¿hay algo de flexibilidad en la tarifa? Sea como sea, nos encantaría avanzar.
