# Vocabulary: words and phrases that signal machine text

**How to use this list.** None of these words is banned. Each one becomes a tell when it (a) stands in for a specific claim the sentence avoided making, or (b) clusters with others from this list. A single "robust" in a statistics paper is a term of art; "robust, seamless, cutting-edge" in one sentence is slop.

Don't fix these by swapping in the synonym from the right-hand column and moving on. That keeps the sentence's skeleton, and the skeleton is what readers notice. Usually the right move is to rewrite the sentence around the specific thing it was avoiding. The "plain alternative" column is for the cases where the word itself is the only problem.

The list drifts with model generations: 2023-era text leans on delve/tapestry/testament, 2025-26 text on quietly/honest/load-bearing. The causes in SKILL.md stay the same, so trust them over the list when you meet a new tic.

## Transitions (fine alone, a tell when stacked)

Moreover, Furthermore, Additionally, In addition, Notably, Importantly, Consequently, Subsequently, Thus, Hence, Indeed, That said, That being said, With that in mind, In essence, Ultimately, Overall.

Plain replacements: and, but, so, also, still, then, or nothing.

## Intensifiers and sincerity markers

truly, genuinely, honestly, really, incredibly, deeply, profoundly, absolutely, extremely, remarkably, undeniably, fundamentally, arguably, simply, literally, super.

Delete first, then reread. If the sentence now feels weak, it needs a fact, not a stronger adverb.
